# Emblèmes de ressources et de travail

Générés avec l’outil ImageGen intégré, puis copiés dans le projet sans modification du bitmap. L’alpha est conservé. Les trois objets de chaque atlas sont recadrés par les remplissages du `.pen` ; les chiffres et libellés restent des calques éditables.

## Ressources — `assets/emblemes-ressources.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game HUD icon atlas. One wide landscape sheet, aspect ratio 3:1, divided conceptually into THREE equal square cells side by side, no visible divisions. Create three beautifully crafted chunky stylized 3D game resource icons with generous transparent padding and absolutely transparent background. They must match a premium cozy wooden software-office management game with warm walnut, moss teal, polished gold, tactile hand-painted materials and dramatic soft rim lighting. LEFT CELL: IMPACT emblem, one large five-point golden star set in a deep emerald faceted jewel medallion with a thick polished brass rim, strong distinctive star silhouette, tiny warm glints, no coins. MIDDLE CELL: TREASURY emblem, a small rich brown leather purse with two or three chunky gold coins peeking out and one gold coin beside it, beautiful seams, no currency symbols. RIGHT CELL: DAY emblem, a small chunky walnut desk flip-calendar with two brass binder rings and a clean warm ivory blank page, a small moss-green tab, no clock hands. Icons viewed slightly from above at a consistent three-quarter orthographic angle, no perspective distortion, similar visual scale and weight. Each object fits entirely in its own third of the image, centers at x 1/6, 1/2, 5/6, y 1/2; each fills about 80 percent of its square cell, no overlap between cells. No ground plane, no backdrop, no frames around the cells, no writing, no text, no letters, no numbers, no mathematical symbols, no logos or watermark. Preserve full alpha transparency between and around the objects. We will add all labels and score numbers as separate editable UI layers.

## Travail — `assets/emblemes-travail.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game work-icon atlas. One wide landscape sheet with aspect ratio 3:1, THREE equal square icon cells left to right, no cell borders. Create premium chunky stylized 3D miniatures that match a cozy software office management game: polished walnut, warm paper, moss teal, soft brass, beautiful tactile painted materials, soft rim light, same angle and scale across all three. LEFT CELL: a short stack of three thick blank ivory task tickets, one teal and one amber divider tab, a big brass paperclip holding them, slightly fanned sheets, reads backlog / work tickets. MIDDLE CELL: an appealing small walnut-backed design sketchbook with a blank cream page, a large teal pencil and a short brass ruler laid diagonally across it, reads design / investigation / research. RIGHT CELL: an appealing compact teal software block with a chunky gold gear attached and a small brass wrench resting against it, reads technical implementation / engineering. Each object must fit inside its own equal square cell, centered at x 1/6, 1/2, 5/6 and y 1/2, fill about 78 percent of the cell with transparent padding. All objects isolated with genuine alpha transparency, no floor, no backdrop, no shadows outside their own cell. Strong readable silhouettes when used as tiny game icons. No people, no labels, no text, no handwriting, no code marks, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no buttons, no interface or watermark.
